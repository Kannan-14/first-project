Chicken Biryani Recipe

Ingredients

For Marination:

  • 500g chicken (bone-in preferred)
  • 1 cup yogurt (curd)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1–2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Juice of ½ lemon

For Rice:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • Whole spices:2 bay leaves, 4 cloves, 4 cardamom, 1-inch cinnamon
  • Salt

Other

  • 2 large onions (thinly sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (optional)
  • 2-3 green chillies
  • ½ cup chopped coriander + mint
  • 3 tbsp oil + 2 tbsp ghee
  • ½ cup milk + pinch of saffron (optional)

Steps

  1. Marinate the Chicken
    • Mix all maination ingredients.
    • Add chicken and coat well.
    • Rest for at least 1 hour (overnight = best flavor).
  2. Cook the Rice (70% done)
    • Wash and soak rice for 20-30 minutes.
    • Boil water wih salt and whole spices.
    • Cook rice until 70% done (still slightly firm).
    • Drain and set aside.
  3. Fry Onions
    • Heat oil and fry sliced onions until goldden and crispy.
    • Remove and set aside (they'll crisp up more as they cool).
  4. Cook the Chicken Base
    • In a heavy pan, heat oil + ghee
    • Add green chilies, tomatoes (optional), and marinated chicken.
    • Cook until chicken is 80% done and gravy thickens
  5. Layer the Biryani
    • Add a layer of rice over chicken
    • Top with:
      • Fried onions
      • Mint & coriander
      • Saffron milk (optional)
      • A little ghee
    • Repeat layers if needed.
  6. Dum Cooking
    • Cover tightly (use dough or tight lid).
    • Cook on low heat for 20–25 minutes.
    • Let it rest 5–10 minutes before opening.

Serve

  • Gently mix layers.
  • Serve hot with raita, salad, or boiled egg.

Tips for Perfect Biryani

  • Use aged basmati rice for long grains.
  • Don’t overcook rice before layering.
  • Cook on very low flame during dum.
  • Add a few drops of kewra water or rose water for aroma.
