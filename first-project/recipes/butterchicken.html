Butter Chicken Recipe



Butter Chicken

Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani) is a rich and creamy North Indian dish made with tender pieces of chicken cooked in a smooth tomato-based gravy. The sauce is flavored with butter, cream, and a blend of aromatic spices, giving it a slightly sweet, mildly spicy taste. Known for its silky texture and vibrant color, butter chicken is best enjoyed with naan, roti, or rice and is one of the most popular Indian dishes worldwide.

Ingredients

  • boneless chicken
  • Greek yogurt
  • ginger-garlic paste
  • Kashmiri chilli powder
  • garam masala
  • cashews
  • tomatoes(or puree)
  • Butter
  • cream
  • Dried fenugreek leaves

Steps to make Butter chicken

  1. Marinate the Chicken
    • Mix all marination ingredients in a bowl.
    • Add chicken and coat well.
    • Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour (overnight is best).
  2. Cook the Chicken
    • Cook the marinated chicken in a pan, oven, or grill until slightly charred and cooked through.
    • Set aside.
  3. Prepare the Gravy
    • Heat butter + oil in a pan.
    • Add onions and saute until golden.
    • Add ginger-garlic paste;cook for 1 minute.
    • Add tomato puree and cook until oil separates.
  4. Add spices
    • Add chilli powder, turmeric, coriander powder,salt.
    • Cook for 2-3 minutes until aromatic.
  5. Combine Chicken & Simmer
    • Add cooked chicken pieces to the gravy.
    • Mix well and simmer for 10 minutes.
  6. Finish with Cream & Flavor
    • Add fresh cream and crushed kasuri methi.
    • Add sugar (optional for slight sweetness).
    • Simmer 2-3 minutes and turn off heat.

Serve

  • Garnish with cream or coriander.
  • Serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.

Tips

  • Use chicken thighs for juicier results.
  • For smoky flavor, add a small piece of charcoal (dhungar method).
  • Adjust cream and butter for richness.
